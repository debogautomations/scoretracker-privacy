Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

10-9-8 ScoreTracker (“the app”), published by Boggy (“we”, “us”), keeps score for the 10-9-8 card game. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What we collect

  • Account details. The username and password you choose when you create an account. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider.
  • Game data you enter. Player names you add, and the games you track: bids, tricks, scores and dates.

The app does not collect your real name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

How we use it

Your data is used only to run the app: to sign you in, and to save and sync your players and game history across your devices. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you across other apps or websites.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on your device and with Supabase, the cloud provider that hosts the app’s database and sign-in. Supabase processes it on our behalf only.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: on the home screen, tap Account, then Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, players and game history from our servers and from your device. You can also delete individual games from History.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
